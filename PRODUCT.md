# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Engineering leads and heads of front-end** (primary) who want their team to use AI coding tools well, not just install them. They arrive from LinkedIn or a referral, skim on a laptop between meetings, and decide whether to book a call.
2. **Hiring managers and recruiters** (secondary) evaluating Vikas for Front-End Lead / senior roles. They want experience, companies, stack, and the CV.

## Product Purpose

Personal site of Vikas Kashyap, Front-End Lead & Full-Stack Engineer in Berlin, 13+ years. It positions him first as a practitioner who helps front-end teams use AI effectively, with his engineering career as the proof. Success: a lead books a call (Calendly) about AI enablement, or a hiring manager downloads the CV / books a call.

## Positioning

Not an "AI influencer". A senior front-end engineer who ships production UI (Natuvion Data Conversion Suite, Tenderize staking dApp, Ticketmaster 3D venues) and builds the AI tooling he works with every day: skills, hooks, review agents, prompts and workflows. His advice comes from using AI in real product work, and from learning from experienced people in the industry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 14 app router, React 18, Tailwind 3, framer-motion, lenis, React Three Fiber are installed.
- Contact: Calendly `https://calendly.com/kashyapvikas20/lets-know-each-other`, email `kashyapvikas20@gmail.com`, Formspree contact form, LinkedIn, GitHub.
- CV download is generated in-browser (`components/download-resume.tsx`, jspdf / react-pdf) from `lib/resume-data.ts`.
- SEO metadata and JSON-LD live in `app/layout.tsx` and `components/structured-data.tsx`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Experience history, 2013 to present: `lib/resume-data.ts`.
- Projects: `lib/projects-data.ts`.
- Company logos: `public/logos/`.
- Personal photos: `public/main.JPG`, `public/IMG_*.jp(e)g`.
- AI mentoring evidence: builds AI tooling in current work; learning by doing and from industry peers.

**Do not fabricate:** workshop counts, number of engineers trained, client names for AI training, testimonials, productivity percentages, "50+ projects", "15+ teams". None of these are confirmed. Skill percentage bars are self-ratings, not evidence.

## Product Principles

1. Practitioner, not preacher: every AI claim is tied to something he builds or ships.
2. Honest scope: say what he does now and offers, never results he has not measured.
3. Proof over adjectives: company names, products, dates and stack beat "exceptional" and "cutting-edge".
4. Two readers, one page: the mentoring offer leads, the career proof follows, and both reach a call.
